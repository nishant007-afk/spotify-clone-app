What it does

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built as a full-featured music player with the look and feel of the app you already love — minus the paywall.

  • 🎵

    Plays every song on your device

    Scans your music library automatically. MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC — whatever you have, it plays it.

  • 📝

    Synced lyrics that follow the music

    Lyrics scroll and highlight in real time as the song plays. Built-in lyrics for 16+ popular tracks.

  • 🔍

    Instant search across your library

    Find songs by title, artist, or album as you type. Browse genres in a color-coded grid.

  • 🔀

    Shuffle, repeat, and full controls

    Shuffle mode, repeat all or repeat one, skip forward/back — everything works the way you expect.

  • 🖼️

    Full-screen player with album art

    Beautiful full-screen player with seek bar, time display, like button, and one-tap lyrics access.

  • 🎧

    Background playback with notifications

    Music keeps playing when you switch apps. Control playback from the notification shade.

  • 📱

    Native Android, smooth performance

    Built with Java and Material Design. Runs on Android 7.0 and above with no lag.

  • 🆓

    100% free. No ads. No catches.

    No premium tier, no subscriptions, no data collection. Open source and transparent.

Get started

Build it yourself.

Open the project in Android Studio and hit build. The whole thing compiles in under a minute.

📦

Source Code

Download the pre-built APK directly, or open the full project in Android Studio.

  1. Open spotify-clone/ in Android Studio
  2. Wait for Gradle sync to finish
  3. Run Build → Build APK
  4. Find APK at app/build/outputs/apk/debug/
  5. Transfer to your phone and install
